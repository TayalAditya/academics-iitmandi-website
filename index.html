Welcome to the Academics section, IIT Mandi


The Academics Section at IIT Mandi is committed to fostering an environment of excellence in education and research. As one of the premier institutes in India, IIT Mandi offers a variety of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in diverse fields of engineering, technology, and sciences. Our academic framework is designed to encourage critical thinking, innovation, and holistic development among students.
The Academics Section also oversees various student initiatives, academic events, and international collaborations that further enrich the learning experience. With a strong focus on both theoretical knowledge and practical application, IIT Mandi is dedicated to shaping the leaders of tomorrow in a dynamic and rapidly evolving global landscape.
