  • FS is given when attendance is below 80%: the student cannot take the end-semester examination and must repeat the course.
  • I is temporary: the remaining part must be completed within about one month of the next regular semester, after which the instructor replaces it with a regular grade; otherwise it becomes F.
  • P (Pass/Fail) credits do not enter the SGPA or CGPA. Up to 6 Pass/Fail credits may be taken in a semester (9 during an approved onsite semester internship).

SGPA and CGPA

SGPA = Σ (ci × gi) / Σ ci, over all courses registered in the semester, where ci is the credits of course i and gi the grade point obtained. F grades are included. The CGPA uses the same formula over all courses taken so far; an F is left out once the course is cleared or replaced by an equivalent course.

The final CGPA on the degree counts every letter-graded credit earned, including credits beyond the minimum, and leaves out F grades once all basket requirements (IC, DC, DE, FE) are met. The F grade stays on the grade sheet and transcript. A CGPA of at least 5.0 is needed to graduate.

Grading used before AY 2024-25 (archive)

Letter grades O, A, B, C, D, E and F carried 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 4 and 0 points; F was the fail grade.

The curriculum of each programme, batch by batch (credit distribution, semester-wise courses and the list of Discipline Electives), is on the Degree Programs page. Every student also completes an internship (industry or research) and the NCC/NSO/NSS/Hiking & Trekking requirement (Ordinance 4.1).

Requirement for Award of B.Tech. (Honours) Degree

IKS + HSS is a basket of the Institute Core: 15 credits for B.Tech. and 12 for B.S. (see section 3). The courses of the basket are in the curriculum of each programme on the Degree Programs page.

  • At most 20 HSS/IKS credits count towards graduation; credits beyond this limit are not counted (UG Ordinance 2026, 4.2.6).
  • HSS/IKS elective credits may count towards a Minor where the Minor allows it (Ordinance 10.4).

Design and Innovation Practicum (DIP) is the hands-on thread of the B.Tech. curriculum. It runs through all four years, from understanding and designing products in the first two years to a major technical project in the final year. At the end of DIP, students are expected to be able to:

  • apply knowledge of mathematics, science and engineering;
  • design and conduct experiments, and analyse and interpret data;
  • design a system, component or process to meet desired needs;
  • work in multi-disciplinary teams;
  • identify, formulate and solve engineering problems;
  • understand professional and ethical responsibility;
  • communicate effectively;
  • understand the impact of engineering solutions in a global and societal context;
  • use the techniques, skills and modern engineering tools needed in engineering practice.

Choosing a DIP problem or project: it should emphasise design, experiments and hands-on skills (not only a collection of published material), allow teamwork and creativity, not be on the critical path of a programme with stringent deadlines, and have concrete, measurable goals and defined criteria for success.

Young Innovator Fellowship (YIF) / Startup Practicum (UG Ordinance 2026, section 13)

YIF is an 18-month programme, run with i-Hub and Catalyst, in which students build a startup of their own. B.Tech. and B.S. students usually begin it in semester 5. A team is preferably one student, or two students with complementary skills (for example from different disciplines); faculty can also propose a project with a student team. The fellowship pays a scholarship of ₹15,000 per month for at most three semesters, allocated to the team as a whole, and the student earns 22 credits over semesters 5, 6 and 7 in place of the courses below.

A minor is intended for a student to gain expertise in an area outside his/her major B.Tech./B.S. discipline. A minor can be in HSS or in any area outside the parent branch; there is no minor in the parent branch. Each minor has a specialist basket of at least 9 credits, which may rest on one or more foundation courses; advanced courses in a basket may rest on basic ones (UG Ordinance 2026, section 10).

  • To complete a minor, a student must earn at least 9 credits (or the total the basket prescribes) from that basket, with a CGPA of 7.0 over the courses counted towards the minor.
  • Common Core, Discipline Core and Discipline Elective courses cannot be counted towards a minor; HSS/IKS elective credits may be counted where the basket permits.
  • Minor courses are counted in the Humanities or Free Elective basket, as the student's curriculum prescribes.
  • A student may take any number of minors, but one course counts towards only one minor. A student cannot take a Second Major and a minor in the same discipline.
  • The name of the minor appears on the transcript and the degree certificate. A minor can be pursued only when its basket is opened and its courses are offered in the registration cycle.

Interdisciplinary skills open many careers, so a B.Tech./B.S. student may add a Second Major in another discipline by spending one extra year on that discipline's core and elective courses. For example, a student of Civil Engineering or Engineering Physics can earn a Second Major in Computer Science and Engineering. The rules below are from the UG Ordinance 2026 (sections 11, 12 and 14).

Second Major

Who can applyCGPA of at least 7.0 and not more than one F grade when applying, at the end of semester 6, with the Faculty Advisor's recommendation. Each school fixes its seats every year and a committee of the Dean (Academics) selects on merit.
Extra coursework36–40 credits of Discipline Core and Discipline Elective courses of the second discipline (a basket set by its School Chair), 196–200 credits in all. These courses cannot be taken Pass/Fail and cannot be courses already counted for the parent degree.
MTP and HonoursMTP must be in the parent discipline (an MTP in the second major counts as Free Electives). Honours applies only to the parent discipline.
Leaving the programmeThere is no voluntary exit. A student with 2 or more F grades in the second-major courses is removed from it; the parent degree is still awarded if its requirements are met.
DegreeB.Tech./B.S. in <Parent Branch> with Second Major in <Secondary Branch>, with one transcript and one CGPA. Medals are competed for with the next graduating batch.

Honours (opt in at the end of semester 4 or 5, with no F grade so far)

  • MTP-1 and MTP-2 (8 credits, in the parent discipline) and DP-301P ISTP, each with grade B or above; no F grade in the programme.
  • Mode A: CGPA of 9.0 or more. Mode B: CGPA of 8.0 or more together with a research article published or accepted in an SCI/SCIE journal, or a patent granted, with IIT Mandi affiliation and declared before the last date of semester-8 grade submission.
  • Degree: B.Tech. (Honours) / B.S. (Honours) in <discipline>. Earlier Honours notification

Specialization

  • Expertise in a specific area of the student's own discipline: at least 15 credits from that specialization's basket, with a CGPA of 7.0 over those courses.
  • Each discipline publishes its list of specializations. The degree reads B.Tech. in <discipline> with Specialization in <area>.
