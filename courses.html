  • FS is given when attendance is below 80%: the student cannot take the end-semester examination and must repeat the course.
  • I is temporary: the remaining part must be completed within about one month of the next regular semester, after which the instructor replaces it with a regular grade; otherwise it becomes F.
  • P (Pass/Fail) credits do not enter the SGPA or CGPA. Up to 6 Pass/Fail credits may be taken in a semester (9 during an approved onsite semester internship).

SGPA and CGPA

SGPA = Σ (ci × gi) / Σ ci, over all courses registered in the semester, where ci is the credits of course i and gi the grade point obtained. F grades are included. The CGPA uses the same formula over all courses taken so far; an F is left out once the course is cleared or replaced by an equivalent course.

The final CGPA on the degree counts every letter-graded credit earned, including credits beyond the minimum, and leaves out F grades once all basket requirements (IC, DC, DE, FE) are met. The F grade stays on the grade sheet and transcript. A CGPA of at least 5.0 is needed to graduate.

Grading used before AY 2024-25 (archive)

Letter grades O, A, B, C, D, E and F carried 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 4 and 0 points; F was the fail grade.

Design and Innovation Practicum (DIP) is the hands-on thread of the B.Tech. curriculum. It runs through all four years, from understanding and designing products in the first two years to a major technical project in the final year. At the end of DIP, students are expected to be able to:

  • apply knowledge of mathematics, science and engineering;
  • design and conduct experiments, and analyse and interpret data;
  • design a system, component or process to meet desired needs;
  • work in multi-disciplinary teams;
  • identify, formulate and solve engineering problems;
  • understand professional and ethical responsibility;
  • communicate effectively;
  • understand the impact of engineering solutions in a global and societal context;
  • use the techniques, skills and modern engineering tools needed in engineering practice.

Choosing a DIP problem or project: it should emphasise design, experiments and hands-on skills (not only a collection of published material), allow teamwork and creativity, not be on the critical path of a programme with stringent deadlines, and have concrete, measurable goals and defined criteria for success.

A minor is intended for a student to gain expertise in an area outside his/her major B.Tech./B.S. discipline. A minor can be in HSS or in any area outside the parent branch; there is no minor in the parent branch. Each minor has a specialist basket of at least 9 credits, which may rest on one or more foundation courses; advanced courses in a basket may rest on basic ones (UG Ordinance 2026, section 10).

  • To complete a minor, a student must earn at least 9 credits (or the total the basket prescribes) from that basket, with a CGPA of 7.0 over the courses counted towards the minor.
  • Common Core, Discipline Core and Discipline Elective courses cannot be counted towards a minor; HSS/IKS elective credits may be counted where the basket permits.
  • Minor courses are counted in the Humanities or Free Elective basket, as the student's curriculum prescribes.
  • A student may take any number of minors, but one course counts towards only one minor. A student cannot take a Second Major and a minor in the same discipline.
  • The name of the minor appears on the transcript and the degree certificate. A minor can be pursued only when its basket is opened and its courses are offered in the registration cycle.

Realizing the current need of interdisciplinary skill development towards successful careers, IIT Mandi has introduced a Double Major program from 2021, by which a student of one discipline is allowed to obtain a second ‘Major’ by spending an extra one or two semesters to study approximately 12 courses of another discipline. For example, a student of Civil Engineering or Engineering Physics would be able to obtain a second major in Computer Science & Engineering. This would enable the student to sit for campus placements for CS related companies as well .
