  • FS is given when attendance is below 80%: the student cannot take the end-semester examination and must repeat the course.
  • I is temporary: the remaining part must be completed within about one month of the next regular semester, after which the instructor replaces it with a regular grade; otherwise it becomes F.
  • P (Pass/Fail) credits do not enter the SGPA or CGPA. Up to 6 Pass/Fail credits may be taken in a semester (9 during an approved onsite semester internship).

SGPA and CGPA

SGPA = Σ (ci × gi) / Σ ci, over all courses registered in the semester, where ci is the credits of course i and gi the grade point obtained. F grades are included. The CGPA uses the same formula over all courses taken so far; an F is left out once the course is cleared or replaced by an equivalent course.

The final CGPA on the degree counts every letter-graded credit earned, including credits beyond the minimum, and leaves out F grades once all basket requirements (IC, DC, DE, FE) are met. The F grade stays on the grade sheet and transcript. A CGPA of at least 5.0 is needed to graduate.

Design and Innovation Practicum

    Design and Innovation Practicum (DIP) is a required course of undergraduate engineering core program. DIP program offers numerous challenges for both faculty and students because of the wide variety of projects in a given year as well as from year to year. At the end of DIP which will span during all the four years of engineering programs in different phases, students are expected to demonstrate the following attributes:

    • Ability to apply knowledge of mathematics, science, and engineering
    • Ability to design and conduct experiments, as well as to analyze and interpret data
    • Ability to design a system, component, or process to meet desired needs
    • Ability to function on multi-disciplinary teams
    • Ability to identify, formulate, and solve engineering problems
    • Understanding of professional and ethical responsibility
    • Ability to communicate effectively
    • Broad education necessary to understand the impact of engineering solutions in a global and societal context
    • Ability to use the techniques, skills, and modern engineering tools necessary for engineering practice

    The program ventures far beyond the traditional classroom instruction, graduating students are beneficiary of a life-long learning process. A successful DIP program can facilitate achieving the educational institutions’ goal of producing the most sought-after graduates by the industry.

    Choosing a DIP problem/project

    • Project that emphasizes design, experimental, and hands-on skills
    • Do not choose a project involving only collection of published materials
    • Problem that allows teamwork and offers opportunity for creativity
    • Project should not be on the critical path of a program with stringent deadlines
    • Project goals must be concrete and measurable
    • The criteria to determine success should be defined

Minor Program

A minor is intended for a student to gain expertise in an area outside his/her major B. Tech discipline. A specialist basket of 3-6 courses is identified for each Minor. Each basket may rest on one or more foundation courses. A basket may have sequences within it, i.e. advanced courses may rest on basic courses in the basket. In order to successfully complete a Minor, a student needs to take at least 9 credits with a CGPA of 7.0 out of the courses defined in that Minor basket. The area of the Minor must be different from the Major discipline of the student; the courses that are part of the common core (including HSS), or the discipline core/electives cannot be counted towards a Minor. A student is allowed to take any number of Minors, but a particular course cannot be counted for more than 1 Minor. The name of the Minor will appear on the Transcript and the degree certificate of the student.

Interdisciplinary skills open many careers, so a B.Tech./B.S. student may add a Second Major in another discipline by spending one extra year on that discipline's core and elective courses. For example, a student of Civil Engineering or Engineering Physics can earn a Second Major in Computer Science and Engineering. The rules below are from the UG Ordinance 2026 (sections 11, 12 and 14).

Second Major

Who can applyCGPA of at least 7.0 and not more than one F grade when applying, at the end of semester 6, with the Faculty Advisor's recommendation. Each school fixes its seats every year and a committee of the Dean (Academics) selects on merit.
Extra coursework36–40 credits of Discipline Core and Discipline Elective courses of the second discipline (a basket set by its School Chair), 196–200 credits in all. These courses cannot be taken Pass/Fail and cannot be courses already counted for the parent degree.
MTP and HonoursMTP must be in the parent discipline (an MTP in the second major counts as Free Electives). Honours applies only to the parent discipline.
Leaving the programmeThere is no voluntary exit. A student with 2 or more F grades in the second-major courses is removed from it; the parent degree is still awarded if its requirements are met.
DegreeB.Tech./B.S. in <Parent Branch> with Second Major in <Secondary Branch>, with one transcript and one CGPA. Medals are competed for with the next graduating batch.

Honours (opt in at the end of semester 4 or 5, with no F grade so far)

  • MTP-1 and MTP-2 (8 credits, in the parent discipline) and DP-301P ISTP, each with grade B or above; no F grade in the programme.
  • Mode A: CGPA of 9.0 or more. Mode B: CGPA of 8.0 or more together with a research article published or accepted in an SCI/SCIE journal, or a patent granted, with IIT Mandi affiliation and declared before the last date of semester-8 grade submission.
  • Degree: B.Tech. (Honours) / B.S. (Honours) in <discipline>. Earlier Honours notification

Specialization

  • Expertise in a specific area of the student's own discipline: at least 15 credits from that specialization's basket, with a CGPA of 7.0 over those courses.
  • Each discipline publishes its list of specializations. The degree reads B.Tech. in <discipline> with Specialization in <area>.
