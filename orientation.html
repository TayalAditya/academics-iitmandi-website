Fee Concession Instructions

For more details, please visit Link.

As per the norms, based on the Family Income, the eligible students (UR/Gen (EWS)/OBC(NCL)) may avail below concession:

1. The most economically backward students (whose family income is less than ₹1 lakh per annum) shall get full remission of tuition fee.

2. The other economically backward students (whose family income is between ₹1 lakh to ₹5 lakh per annum) shall get remission of 2/3rd of tuition fee.

3. For availing above fee concession, please submit valid family income certificate issued by the authorized officials in Revenue Deptt./DC/SDM/Tehsildar, etc. (issued on or after 1st April, 2026) alongwith the notarized Affidavit.

4. The family income certificate and affidavit should be submitted on the SAMARTH portal while making remaining payment and/or updating the fee details.

5. If income certificate and affidavit are not submitted during the above-mentioned duration, no fee concession will be permitted and the student shall pay full fee (except SC/ST/PwD candidates).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) – Anti-Ragging Affidavits

  1. Are all formats required to be printed on Non-Judicial Stamp Paper?
    Answer: No. Only the Anti-Ragging Affidavits (Annexure I & Annexure II) are required to be executed on Rs. 10 Non-Judicial Stamp Paper. All other admission documents may be submitted in the prescribed format unless specifically instructed otherwise.

  2. Should the Anti-Ragging Affidavits be prepared on Non-Judicial Stamp Paper or plain paper? What should be the denomination/value of the Stamp Paper?
    Answer: The Anti-Ragging Affidavits (Annexure I & Annexure II) must be prepared on Rs. 10 Non-Judicial Stamp Paper (valid in the State of Himachal Pradesh).

  3. Can the Anti-Ragging Affidavits be prepared in our local jurisdiction, or do they need to be executed in Mandi (Himachal Pradesh)?
    Answer: The affidavits may be prepared either in your local jurisdiction or in Mandi. However, the minimum denomination of the Non-Judicial Stamp Paper should be valid as per the rules of the state where the affidavit is executed.

  4. Who is authorized to attest the Anti-Ragging Affidavits?
    Answer: The affidavits should preferably be attested by an Oath Commissioner. In case an Oath Commissioner is not available, attestation by a Notary Public is also acceptable.
